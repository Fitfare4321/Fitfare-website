3. Part A — FitFare user app

Applies to the FitFare Android app (package com.example.fitfare) and any future iOS build of the same user app.

A1. Account & profile

  • Name, phone number, email address — to create your account, confirm bookings, and contact you about a booking.
  • Gender — collected once during onboarding and editable later; used to personalise recommendations and to filter centres with gender-specific access rules.
  • Date of birth — optional; used for age eligibility (18+) and personalisation.
  • Profile photo — optional; you choose it from your gallery. It is stored in our cloud storage and shown only in your app profile.
  • City and preferred activities (for example gym, yoga, boxing, pilates) — used to personalise discovery.

A2. Authentication

  • Firebase Phone Authentication — we send an OTP to your phone number and store the resulting session/ID token state. Google may perform an in-app device integrity or reCAPTCHA check to prevent OTP abuse.
  • Google Sign-In (if you choose it) — we receive your basic Google profile (name, email, photo).
  • We never see or store your Google password.

A3. Location

  • Approximate and precise location — used to show gyms near you, sort results by distance, and display travel distance.
  • Background location — the permission may be requested for continued nearby-centre discovery. It is optional; core booking works without it.
  • Reverse geocoding — coordinates may be converted into a readable area/city name for display.
  • Location is collected only after you grant permission and can be revoked anytime in Android settings. We do not sell or share your location with advertisers.

A4. Bookings, slots & QR check-in

  • Centre booked, service and slot, booking date and time, quantity, selected activities, amount, payment method, and booking status.
  • QR check-in events and attendance timestamps at the Partner centre.
  • These details are visible to the Partner gym you booked (see Sharing).

A5. Fit Credits

  • Prepaid Fit Credit balance, credit lots and their expiry, amounts held in escrow against a booking, and transaction history.
  • Credit transfers: when you send Fit Credits, we look up the recipient by the phone number you enter and confirm only whether a FitFare account exists and its display name. Both sender and recipient receive a transaction record and may receive a notification.

A6. Favourites, reviews & in-app activity

  • Saved / favourite centres.
  • Ratings and written reviews you post for a centre. Reviews are shown publicly in the app with your display name.
  • Search terms and browsing inside the app, used to operate and improve discovery.

A7. Payments

  • Payments and Fit Credit top-ups are processed by Razorpay.
  • FitFare stores only payment metadata: amount, order id, payment id, verification signature, status, and the related booking or credit lot.
  • Card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN and net-banking credentials are never stored on FitFare servers.

A8. Notifications

  • We store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token linked to your account so we can send transactional alerts — for example booking confirmed, or Fit Credits received.
  • Tokens are removed when you log out, unregister the device, or schedule account deletion.
  • You can disable notifications in Android settings at any time; bookings still work.

A9. Camera & photos

  • Camera — used only when you open the QR scanner to check in at a centre. Camera frames are processed on-device to read the QR code; we do not record video.
  • Photos / media — used only when you pick a profile picture.

A10. Device, diagnostics & on-device storage

  • Device model, OS version, app version, and basic error/performance logs needed for reliability and security.
  • On your device we cache non-sensitive data (for example recent searches, onboarding state, cached images and a cached credits/profile snapshot) so the app opens quickly. Clearing app data removes this cache.
  • Remote configuration flags are fetched to enable or disable features; these contain no personal data.

A11. Communications you send us

  • Support emails and messages you choose to send, including any screenshots you attach.
  • If in-app assistant features are enabled, the prompts you submit are processed to generate a response.

4. Part B — FitFare Partner app

Applies to the FitFare Partner app used by gym and studio owners/operators. Partner users must be 18+ and represent a legitimate business.

B1. Account

  • Owner/business email, phone, and Firebase authentication state (OTP / sign-in).

B2. Identity & KYC documents

  • Owner name; government identity document type and images — Aadhaar, Passport, or Driving Licence.
  • PAN and PAN type.
  • Business registration documents as applicable — GST certificate, Shops & Establishment licence, Udyam registration, certificate of incorporation, LLP or partnership deed.
  • Optional cancelled cheque or bank passbook image.

These are used strictly for identity verification, fraud prevention, payout eligibility, and legal/tax compliance. Access is restricted to authorised FitFare reviewers and systems.

B3. Bank & payout data

  • Account holder name, bank account number, and IFSC. Account numbers are encrypted at rest.
  • Bank verification status. We may initiate a ₹1 micro-deposit (“penny drop”) through RazorpayX to validate that the account is valid and belongs to you.
  • Payout statements, settlement amounts, commission, and adjustments.

B4. Business & operations data

  • Centre profile: name, address and geo-coordinates, photos, amenities, operating hours, services, slots, trainers, and pricing you publish.
  • Bookings received, QR check-in and attendance records, no-shows, and support messages.

B5. Notifications, media & diagnostics

  • FCM device tokens for operational alerts such as a new booking or a KYC status update.
  • Camera and photo library — to capture or upload KYC and centre images, and to save your centre QR image to Photos if you choose.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability diagnostics, plus app version info used for in-app update prompts.

B6. What Partners see about users

Partners receive only what is needed to honour a visit: booking reference, display name, booking date/time, service booked, quantity, and check-in status. Partners do not receive your payment credentials, Fit Credit balance, or full account profile. Partners must not reuse or sell user data.

8. Retention

  • Account and booking records — kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for disputes, accounting, and fraud prevention.
  • Payment and payout records — kept as long as tax, audit, and chargeback rules require.
  • Partner KYC and bank documents — kept while the partnership is active; after offboarding they are deleted or anonymised subject to legal retention periods.
  • Push tokens — removed on logout, device unregister, or deletion request.
  • Support messages — kept for a reasonable period to maintain service history.

9. Account deletion

User app

In the app go to Profile → Edit Profile → Delete my account.

  • Your deletion is scheduled with a 30-day hold and you are signed out.
  • If you sign in again within 30 days, the deletion is automatically cancelled and your account continues as normal.
  • After 30 days, an automated job permanently deletes or anonymises your account identifiers (name, email, phone, photo, gender, date of birth, preferences), removes your push tokens, and deletes your authentication record.
  • Transaction, payout, and tax records that we must legally retain may be kept in de-identified or minimal form.

Partner app

Request deletion from the Partner app profile screen, or email collaborations@fitfare.in from your registered address. Pending settlements are reconciled first, and KYC/financial records are retained where law requires.

Either app

You can always email collaborations@fitfare.in and we will action a verified deletion request.

10. Security

  • HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit.
  • Token-based authentication; server-side authorisation on every account-scoped API.
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive Partner bank fields; restricted storage buckets for KYC documents.
  • Role-based access for admin review tools and audit logging of sensitive actions.
  • Payment credentials are handled by Razorpay under PCI-compliant processes and never touch our servers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Keep your device, email, and OTP access protected.

11. Your rights (including DPDP) & grievance

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and request a summary of processing;
  • Correct or update your profile — most fields are editable in-app;
  • Request erasure of your personal data (see Account deletion);
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based — for example revoke location, camera, photos, or notification permissions in device settings;
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, as provided under DPDP;
  • Raise a grievance about how your data is handled.

Send requests to collaborations@fitfare.in. We may verify your identity before acting, and we respond within the period required by applicable law. If you are not satisfied, you may escalate to the relevant data protection authority.

12. Children

FitFare is intended for people aged 18 or older (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used FitFare, contact us and we will delete the data.

13. International transfers

Your data may be processed on servers in India or in other countries where our infrastructure providers operate. Where data is transferred across borders, we take steps reasonably designed to protect it in line with applicable law.
